Epidemiological suite

Sample size, power and analysis for community medicine, epidemiology and clinical research.

Starting the app in your browser…

Did you know?

What's inside

  • 215 study designs in 67 sample-size and power calculators
  • A guide from your research question to the design and its calculator
  • Analysis of counts you already have, effect-size converters, a glossary
  • Studies: your calculations kept together, as a Word file
Everything runs in your browser: nothing you type is sent to a server.

How to cite

Maheshwari, M. (2026). Epidemiological suite (Version 1.0.0) [Computer software]. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23794230

Other formats (BibTeX, RIS) are in the app's Cite tab. Please also cite the method each calculator names.
